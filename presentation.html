Biophysical Equations

Mathematical Formulation: Extended Bergman Minimal Model

Calibrated with Indian Phenotypic carbohydrate absorption kinetics

Coupled Differential Equation System

dG/dt = -(p1·η_met + X + E_uptake)·G(t) + p1·Gb + Ra(t)/Vg - SGLT2_dump/Vg
dX/dt = -p2·X(t) + p3·max(0, I(t) - Ib)
dI/dt = -n·(I(t) - Ib) + β_reserve·γ·max(0, G(t) - h)

GI Meal Absorption Function Ra(t)

Ra(t) = [Carbs_mg / (t_max)²] · Δt · exp(-Δt / t_max)

t_max is dynamically modulated by Glycemic Index (GI): High-GI staples (polished rice, naan) produce rapid peak absorption, while low-GI millets and fenugreek delay t_max by 40%.

Cardiovascular 3-Element Windkessel

dP/dt = Q(t)/C - (P(t) - Pv) / (Rp · C)

Arterial compliance (C) and pulse wave velocity (PWV) reflect central aortic stiffening, modeling the accelerated microvascular shear stress on renal glomeruli and retinal capillaries.
